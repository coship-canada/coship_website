### What types of vehicles are eligible for shipping?
We can ship most vehicles that fit into standard containers, with the exception of Electric Vehicles (EVs) and Hybrid vehicles due to safety regulations.
### What type of visa is required for vehicle shipping? Can Canadian citizens or permanent residents apply?
Requirements vary by case.
- Visa Holders (Study/Work Permit, etc.): If you have a Canadian visa authorized for a stay of 1 year or more as of the customs clearance date, you may import and use the vehicle under temporary customs clearance.

### Are tuned or modified vehicles accepted?
We do not recommend shipping modified or tuned vehicles as they may cause issues during the mandatory registration inspections.

# Vehicle Transport FAQ
##### Car shipping, import, export, and customs-related questions.
### What is a "Mover"?
According to Korea Customs Service standards, a "Mover" is someone entering Korea for the purpose of relocating their residence. This includes Korean nationals who have stayed abroad for 1 year or more, or foreign nationals/overseas Koreans who plan to stay in Korea for 1 year or more. (Please refer to the main guide on the page for detailed criteria.)
### How many vehicles can be cleared as moving goods?
Moving goods status is recognized for a maximum of one vehicle per individual or family.
### Does the vehicle need to be registered/used in my name for more than 3 months? (Can I ship a brand-new car?)
You must prove that the vehicle was registered in your name (or a family member's name) and used for at least 3 months (e.g., ICBC Vehicle Registration). Vehicles owned for less than 3 months cannot be cleared as moving goods.
### Can a vehicle registered under another family member's name be shipped as moving goods?
If you are registered as the user of the vehicle and have used it for more than 3 months, and you qualify as a "Mover," it can be recognized as moving goods.
### If the VIN starts with "K," is it tax-exempt? (Are all Korean cars tax-free?)
Vehicles produced in Korea (VIN starting with 'K') may be eligible for customs duty exemption. However, Korean brand vehicles that were not manufactured in Korea are not eligible for this exemption.
### Can I ship pickup trucks like the F-150?
Pickup trucks are classified as freight vehicles rather than passenger cars, so they cannot be cleared as moving goods. If you wish to ship a pickup truck, we can assist through the general import procedure.
### Can I ship electric (EV) or hybrid vehicles?
Vehicles with high-capacity rechargeable batteries are rejected by shipping lines due to the high risk of fire during transit. Therefore, we are unable to accept these vehicles for shipping.
### Can I ship a vehicle with a remaining loan or a leased vehicle?
- Loan: Shipping is only possible if the customer has 100% ownership, as issues arise during export declaration in the origin country if a lien exists.
- Lease: Acceptance is possible if there is proof that you used the vehicle for at least 3 months and that the title was ultimately transferred to your name.
### How long does shipping take? (Estimated Lead Time)
- Vancouver > Korea: 6 to 8 weeks (4 to 6 weeks using Express service).
- Toronto > Korea: 8 to 10 weeks.
- ※ Subject to change based on carrier schedules, port conditions, weather, and inland transportation status.
### What is the most common way to take a car from Canada to Korea?
We load your vehicle inside a container, secure it safely (shoring), and transport it to the destination.
### Can I put other belongings/luggage inside the car?
In principle, placing other moving goods or luggage inside the vehicle is strictly restricted.
### Where can I check the estimated taxes?
You can check the estimated tax for moving goods vehicles on the official Korea Customs Service calculator or through their online Q&A. Please note that actual taxes incurred during clearance may differ.
- Official Korea Customs Service Car Tax Calculator
  LINK: Official Korea Customs Service Car Tax Calculator -> https://www.customs.go.kr/kcs/ad/tax/CarTaxCalculation.do
### Are general imports (for commercial purposes) or short-term travel shipping possible?
Any vehicle that can be loaded into a container can be shipped by COSHIP (Canada Shipping). For general imports, customers must personally verify subsequent procedures like clearance and inspection. For travel purposes, we can assist after a consultation.
### Can I ship a temporarily imported vehicle back to Korea?
Yes. We provide shipping services for sending vehicles back to Korea after you have finished your stay in Canada.
### What support is provided before leaving Canada (BC standards)?
We support ICBC insurance cancellation, guidance/agency for license plate returns, agency for receiving insurance refunds, and export declaration procedures.
### Are inspection and registration 100% guaranteed after arrival in Korea?
Please understand that we cannot be held responsible for various reasons for failure during vehicle inspection or problems discovered after customs clearance.

# Courier FAQ
##### Small parcel, air, and ocean shipping questions.
### Will there be taxes or duties upon arrival in Canada?
Yes. Depending on the item type, declared value, and quantity, customs duties, taxes, or clearance fees may be applied by the Canadian authorities.
### How does the customs clearance process work for Ocean Freight?
You have two options for clearance in Canada:
- Self-Clearance: You visit a CBSA office personally for an interview to clear your goods.
- Brokerage Service: A customs broker handles the process for you (brokerage fees and taxes may apply).
### What items are prohibited when shipping from Korea to Canada?
Certain items are restricted, including meat products/certain foods, hazardous materials (explosives, flammables), cash, jewelry, and counterfeit goods. Regulations vary by item, so please check with us before shipping.
### Can I ship food or health supplements from Korea?
Some foods and supplements are allowed, but they are subject to strict Canadian customs regulations and inspections. Meat-based products and certain agricultural items are strictly restricted.
### How can I ship a parcel from Korea to Canada?
Once you book your shipment, we provide free doorstep pickup anywhere in Korea. Your items are then sent to our Korea branch, transported to Canada, cleared through customs, and delivered directly to your Canadian address.
### How long does shipping from Korea to Canada take?
Air Express takes an average of 3–5 business days, while Ocean Freight takes an average of 5–7 weeks. Choose Air for speed and Ocean for multiple boxes or moving goods.
### How are shipping costs calculated from Korea?
Costs depend on weight, volume, item type, origin, and shipping method. For Air Express, the rate is based on the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight. Please contact us for a detailed quote.
### Is pickup available throughout Korea?
Yes. We offer free pickup service in most areas across South Korea, so you do not need to carry heavy packages to a courier location.
Free Pickup Requirements
• Maximum weight of 20 kg per box
• Total dimensions (length + width + height) must not exceed 150 cm
*Additional charges may apply for Jeju Island and certain remote or island regions.
### Is door-to-door delivery available in Canada?
Yes. We provide seamless door-to-door service from Korea to Canada. In the Vancouver and Toronto areas, COSHIP staff handle deliveries personally; for other regions, delivery is completed via UPS.
### Can I pick up my parcel directly at a Canada branch?
Yes. If you are unable to receive delivery at home, you can pick up your items at the following locations:
- Vancouver (Coquitlam HQ)
- Lougheed (Courier Hub)
- Toronto (Mississauga Branch)
### Can I track my shipment from Korea?
Yes. For Air Express, a UPS tracking number will be emailed to you for real-time tracking. For Ocean Freight, we provide email updates at key stages: vessel departure, arrival in Canada, and customs clearance completion.
### When should I use Ocean Freight instead of Air?
Ocean Freight is ideal if you are shipping multiple boxes (such as moving goods or immigration bags) or if you want to save on shipping costs compared to air transport.
### How should I pack my items for shipping from Korea?
Since international shipping involves long-distance transit, sturdy packing is essential. We recommend using heavy-duty boxes, plenty of cushioning materials (bubble wrap), and packing items individually for maximum protection.
### Is there a limit on the number of boxes I can send?
There is no absolute limit, but we recommend choosing the service that fits your volume. Air Express is best for 1–2 small boxes. Ocean Freight is commonly used for up to 7 immigration bags. For volumes larger than that, our professional international moving service may be more appropriate.
### Can I use plastic moving boxes for air shipping?
We recommend using standard cardboard boxes for air shipping. Please note that plastic moving boxes require special handling due to air freight size and loading regulations, which incurs an additional fee of 20,000 KRW per box.
