# International Moving FAQ
##### Find answers about overseas moving between Canada and Korea. Customs, Packing, transit time, and delivery questions.
### When is the best time to start preparing for my international move?
You are welcome to contact us for a consultation and estimate at any time. However, to ensure a smooth process, we highly recommend requesting a quote at least 1–2 months prior to your scheduled departure.
For the most accurate planning, it is best to proceed once you have received your Visa Invitation Letter or your actual arrival date is confirmed. Please note that transit times can vary significantly depending on your specific departure or destination region in Canada, so early inquiries are strongly advised.
### How are moving costs calculated?
Moving costs are determined based on the total volume (CBM) of your cargo. Final pricing may vary depending on the working environment, delivery location, and the specific items being packed.
### Can I book my move if my final destination address is not yet decided?
Yes, absolutely. You can start the moving process using a temporary address for the initial customs clearance. Once your final delivery address is confirmed, simply notify your coordinator. To give you extra time to settle in, we provide one month of free warehouse storage.
