## Our Story
Growth Driven by Customer Stories
Many of COSHIP’s services were not created through market analysis, but through real customer requests.
Our sea small-cargo shipping service began when a student asked how to send a small amount of personal belongings back home. This later expanded into air courier services.
Freight forwarding services grew from business customers requesting support with import shipments, and our 3PL fulfillment services developed naturally alongside clients launching and scaling their online businesses.
More recently, as interest in K-culture has rapidly expanded, demand from Canadian customers purchasing products directly from Korean online stores has increased significantly. In response, COSHIP introduced a cross-border e-commerce logistics service that connects direct air shipments from Korea with last-mile delivery across Canada through Canada Post.
In this way, COSHIP’s services have always evolved in response to customer needs and changing market environments.
To learn more about how each service began, please visit our Our Story page.

# FAQ
BTN: View All FAQs -> https://www.coship.ca/international-moving-faq
### When is the best time to start preparing for my international move?
You are welcome to contact us for a consultation and estimate at any time. However, to ensure a smooth process, we highly recommend requesting a quote at least 1–2 months prior to your scheduled departure.
For the most accurate planning, it is best to proceed once you have received your Visa Invitation Letter or your actual arrival date is confirmed. Please note that transit times can vary significantly depending on your specific departure or destination region in Canada, so early inquiries are strongly advised.
### How are moving costs calculated?
Moving costs are determined based on the total volume (CBM) of your cargo. Final pricing may vary depending on the working environment, delivery location, and the specific items being packed.
### Can I book my move if my final destination address is not yet decided?
Yes, absolutely. You can start the moving process using a temporary address for the initial customs clearance. Once your final delivery address is confirmed, simply notify your coordinator. To give you extra time to settle in, we provide one month of free warehouse storage.

## Check these items before your international move
### Pre-Packing Checklist
- Passport Validity: Please check the expiration dates of all family members traveling. If a passport has less than 6 months of validity remaining, please apply for a renewal in advance and send a copy of the photo page to your COSHIP representative.
- Item Sorting: Please organize and separate your belongings in advance to ensure that items being shipped overseas do not get mixed up with items staying behind.
- Elevator Reservations: If you live in an apartment or condo, you may need to notify the management office (in Korea) or the building manager (in Canada) of your moving schedule to reserve the elevator.
- Refrigerator Preparation: If you are shipping a refrigerator, we recommend disconnecting the power and cleaning the interior at least 3 days before packing to ensure it is completely dry.
- Parking Space: Please secure a parking space for the moving truck on the day of the move.
- Valuables & Essentials: Please keep passports, air tickets, important documents, jewelry, and cash separately so they are not accidentally packed with the general household shipment.
### Post-Packing Checklist
- Departure Updates: If your travel plans change, please notify your COSHIP representative of your final departure date as soon as possible.
- Delivery Details: Double-check and confirm your local address, contact information, move-in date, and preferred delivery date with your coordinator.
- Address Not Confirmed? If your final destination address is not yet settled, you may provide a friend or acquaintance's contact information as a temporary alternative.
- Cargo Insurance: If you wish to purchase shipping insurance, please consult your representative before the shipment leaves to confirm eligibility and complete the necessary procedures.
### Pre-Delivery Checklist (Local Destination)
- Elevator Reservations: Once your delivery date is confirmed following customs clearance, please check with your condo or apartment management to reserve the elevator.
- Structural Details & Access: Please notify your COSHIP coordinator in advance if your residence has any of the following specific structural features:
- Townhouse: Narrow hallways or living spaces starting from the second floor.
- Condo: Parking/loading zones located far from the entrance, or buildings without elevators.
- House: Heavy items needing to be moved to the second floor or basement, or unusual staircase layouts.
- Storage Requests: If you are unable to receive your shipment immediately upon arrival, please inform your representative before the cargo is dispatched.
- Note: Depending on the region and situation, additional storage fees may apply.

Final duty-free eligibility is determined by customs review and cannot be guaranteed by COSHIP. We provide guidance based on current regulations and your individual situation.

Customs warehouse fees and related charges apply in Korea. Detailed costs and procedures will be explained during consultation.
## Check these items before your international move
### Before Packing Checklist
☐ Check the passport validity of all family members traveling abroad
☐ If any passport has less than 6 months remaining, renew it and send the photo page to your COSHIP representative
☐ Separate items to be shipped from items to be left behind
☐ If you live in an apartment or condo, confirm move notice and elevator reservation requirements
☐ If shipping a refrigerator, unplug and empty it about 3 days before packing
☐ Secure truck parking space in advance on moving day
☐ Keep passports, tickets, important documents, jewelry, and cash separate from the shipment
### After Packing Checklist
☐ Inform your COSHIP representative if your departure schedule changes
☐ Confirm and provide your final address, contact number, move-in date, and preferred delivery date
☐ If your address is not confirmed, provide a friend’s or relative’s contact information
☐ If you require cargo insurance, contact your representative before shipment for availability and instructions
### Before Local Delivery Checklist
☐ Confirm elevator reservation requirements if you live in an apartment or condo
☐ Check if a ladder truck is required (additional cost may apply)
☐ Inform your representative in advance if any of the following apply:
Parking or loading area is far from the entrance or no elevator is available
Heavy items must be moved to upper floors, basement, or via unusual stairs
Truck cannot access directly in front of the residence (e.g., narrow streets)
☐ Notify your representative in advance if you cannot receive the shipment immediately (storage fees may apply)
