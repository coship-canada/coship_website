# 3PL Fulfillment FAQ
##### Warehousing, pick & pack, inventory, and order fulfillment.
### What exactly is 3PL Fulfillment?
3PL (Third-Party Logistics) Fulfillment is a service where a professional logistics company handles inventory storage, order processing (picking/packing), shipping, and returns management on your behalf. COSHIP provides integrated support, from international transportation to local fulfillment within Canada.
### What makes COSHIP 3PL different from standard 3PL providers?
COSHIP directly operates its own CBSA-certified bonded warehouse. While standard 3PLs often require goods to be moved to a separate facility after customs clearance, COSHIP handles both clearance and storage in-house. This allows for immediate outbound readiness and eliminates unnecessary transit time, delays, and additional costs.
### Is there a minimum volume requirement?
Volume requirements can vary based on product type and operational methods. Please share your estimated monthly order volume and inbound quantities, and we will provide a feasibility assessment and a customized quote.
### What is required to get started?
Simply share your sales channels, product information (dimensions/weight), and estimated monthly volume. We offer rapid onboarding via account sharing, requiring no complex technical development.
### How do I request a quote?
Please share your sales channels, product details, and estimated monthly volume through the Request a Quote form on our website. A dedicated representative will review your information and contact you shortly.
